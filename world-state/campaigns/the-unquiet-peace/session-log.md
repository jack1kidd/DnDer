# Session Log - The Unquiet Peace

*9th of Sun's Height, 3E 247. Larenne, Glenumbra, High Rock.*

Third Era Tamriel, generations before the Camoran Usurper crisis and centuries before
any Dagon invasion — the unremarkable middle of the Septim Peace. D&D 5e chassis by
explicit player request, Elder Scrolls flavor throughout (never named in generic D&D
terms at the table). Voice: Ursula K. Le Guin — measured, anthropological, patient
with custom and consequence.

**The PC** is an Argonian doorsman at the Hollow Horn, a coaching inn on Larenne's
market square. He begins at Level 0 — no class, no magic, no combat training, an
ordinary working man. Full mechanical detail (magicka, the Ladder of Training, the
Whispering School of Illusion) lives in `ruleset.json`; nothing on that ladder is ever
granted for free, only earned through real in-fiction training.

**Starting location:** The Hollow Horn, Larenne.

**Key NPCs at the Horn:** Perrine Aubrec (innkeeper, hired the PC herself), Nolwenn
Aubrec (her niece, runs the kitchen), Toussaint Merigot ("Touss," head ostler, the one
man in town who's never called the PC "the Argonian").

**Beyond the Horn:** Sister Ysolt Meaune (chapterhouse infirmary), Brother Aumery (the
chapterhouse's chronicler and this campaign's in-world illustrator), Baroness Isabeau
Vaudry (holds the Barony of Larenne), Perrin Guisse (stationer, sells a defective
Illusion primer — and is walking proof of what a bad self-taught working costs), Ysolt
Fauvel (the town's charm-wife, who could teach the closing but not Illusion itself).

**The three plot tiers:**
- *Local:* the ordinary daily texture of the Hollow Horn and Larenne — its people,
  its small grievances, its customs. This is the campaign's real engine.
- *Regional:* the Barony's hearth-roll dispute, the Fellmarsh-adjacent moor concerns,
  and whatever slow thread the player chooses to pull at (a rumor about the shut-up
  Dower House, a coaching-road job, a chapterhouse curiosity).
- *World (GM-eyes-only, never rushed):* the PC is, unknown to himself, a Shadowscale —
  and has explicitly asked to earn his way toward mastery (Illusion magic, and a
  personality shift toward calm and ruthless) through real play, not a handout. See
  `world-bible.json` `hidden_truths` and `ruleset.json`'s pacing discipline before
  narrating anything uncanny about him. Default cadence: nothing, most sessions.
